## Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
*Government & Policy*

**Listeria Recall Hits Imported Pork Products — 1,500 Pounds Pulled from Distribution**
Published: 2026-09-06 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766384)

Prime Line Distributors and Ferrarini USA recalled approximately 1,513 pounds of imported ready-to-eat pork guanciale due to possible Listeria monocytogenes contamination. The products were distributed from Florida and New Jersey facilities. While no illnesses have been reported in CDC feeds, Listeria poses serious risk to pregnant women (can cause miscarriage, stillbirth, and preterm delivery) and young children.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Quick-hit safety news for pregnancy and parenting outlets: "Listeria Recall Alert: What Pregnant Women and Parents Need to Know About Deli Meats." Evergreen service hook — remind readers that Listeria risk extends beyond the obvious (hot dogs, soft cheese) to cured meats like guanciale. Good fit for Consumer Reports, Parents safety column, or a parenting blog's "recall roundup."
