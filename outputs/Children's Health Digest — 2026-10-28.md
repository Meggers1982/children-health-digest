## Family Voices

**Federal Court Blocks HHS Integration Mandate for People with Disabilities — Major Implications for Children's Home and Community-Based Services**
Published: 2026-10-28 | Source: Family Voices | [Link](https://familyvoices.org/what-to-know-about-the-texas-v-kennedy-decision/)

A federal court in Texas issued a final judgment in *Texas v. Kennedy*, siding with states that challenged the 2024 HHS update to Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act. The ruling strikes down the "integration mandate" that required states to provide services in the most integrated setting appropriate for people with disabilities. For families of children with disabilities and complex medical needs, this could affect access to home and community-based services versus institutional care — a critical issue for children who rely on Medicaid waivers for in-home nursing, therapies, and supports.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, Romper, or disability-focused outlets like The Mighty: "What the Texas v. Kennedy ruling means for families of children with disabilities" — an explainer on how this court decision could affect your child's access to home-based care, therapies, and community services. Focus on real-world impact: could more kids be pushed toward institutional settings?
- **Trade/B2B:** For Pediatric News or health policy outlets: analysis of how the ruling may reshape state Medicaid waiver programs and pediatric home health services — and what pediatricians should tell families navigating care planning now.

Published: 2026-10-27 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/elections/midterm-election-update-immigrant-health-policy/)

KFF's midterm election analysis documents how immigration enforcement policies are causing some immigrant families to avoid medical care and drop health coverage — including for U.S. citizen children who are fully eligible for Medicaid and CHIP. **KFF Survey: Majority of Women in Abortion-Ban States Don't Know Where to Access Care — Up 10 Points Since 2024**
Published: 2026-10-26 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/womens-health-policy/survey-more-than-half-of-women-in-states-with-abortion-bans-wouldnt-know-where-to-get-an-abortion-if-they-needed-one-up-since-2024/)

A new KFF survey finds 53% of reproductive-age women in states with abortion bans would not know where to go or find information if they needed an abortion — up from 43% in 2024. The information gap is widening two-plus years post-Dobbs, with implications for maternal health, pregnancy complications, and family planning across affected states.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** For The Cut, Romper, or women's health outlets: "The post-Dobbs information gap is getting worse, not better" — a reported piece on why so many women are in the dark and what resources exist. Include practical guidance for readers in affected states.
