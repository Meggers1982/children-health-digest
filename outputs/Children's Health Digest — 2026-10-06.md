**New AI Tool Uses Language Patterns to Predict Suicide Risk in Crisis Conversations**
Published: 2026-10-05 | Source: Child Mind Institute | [Link](https://childmind.org/blog/using-large-language-models-to-create-lexicons-for-interpretable-text-models-with-high-content-validity-the-suicide-risk-lexicon/)

Researchers have developed a language-processing tool that analyzes words and phrases in text conversations to estimate suicide risk. Tested on crisis counseling conversations, the "Suicide Risk Lexicon" accurately predicted risk and identified which language patterns were most strongly associated with imminent danger. The tool is designed to be interpretable — meaning clinicians can see why it flagged a conversation.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** "Could AI help spot when a teen is in crisis? What parents need to know about new text-analysis tools" — pitch to Parents, NYT Well, or a teen mental health outlet. Frame around what this could mean for crisis texting services and school-based interventions, with caveats about privacy and accuracy.
- **Trade/B2B:** Analysis for Pediatric News or Contemporary Pediatrics on clinical applications of NLP-based suicide risk detection in pediatric settings — including implementation considerations and limitations.

Dr. Amy Margolis of the Child Mind Institute's Scientific Research Council explains that Nonverbal Learning Disorder (NVLD) — marked by poor spatial reasoning, difficulty reading social cues, and trouble with visual-motor tasks — often goes unrecognized in children and adults.
