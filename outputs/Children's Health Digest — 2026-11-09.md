## Georgetown University Center for Children and Families
*Advocacy & Nonprofits*

**2.56 million children have lost Medicaid/CHIP coverage since January 2025 — and many are also losing SNAP**
Published: 2026-11-09 | Source: Georgetown Center for Children and Families (CCF) | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/11/09/millions-of-low-income-children-are-losing-both-medicaid-chip-coverage-and-snap-benefits/)

Georgetown's latest analysis of state administrative data through August 2026 shows child enrollment in Medicaid and CHIP has dropped by 2.56 million since January 2025. The report highlights a compounding crisis: many of these same families are simultaneously losing SNAP (food assistance) benefits, creating a dual coverage gap that threatens both healthcare access and food security for low-income children.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to NYT Well, Parents, or Today's Parent as an urgent explainer for low-income families: "Is your child still covered? What the Medicaid enrollment drop means for your family — and how to check your status before you need care." Include a checklist of warning signs (missed renewal notices, state letters) and steps to re-enroll. Could also frame as a deep-dive investigative piece on what's driving the losses — administrative churn, policy changes, or outreach failures.
- **Trade/B2B:** Strong angle for health policy trades (Health Affairs blog, KFF-style analysis) and pediatric practice publications: What are safety-net pediatricians seeing in their patient panels? Are kids showing up uninsured for well-child visits? Frame as a practice management and policy advocacy piece for Contemporary Pediatrics or Pediatric News.

## Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
*Government & Policy*

**Listeria recall: Fresh To You brand breakfast sandwiches pulled over contamination risk to young children**
Published: 2026-11-08 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766813)

Fresh & Ready Foods LLC is recalling 2,414 units of its Fresh To You Sausage, Egg & Cheese Biscuit Sandwiches (4.6 oz) due to potential Listeria monocytogenes contamination. The CDC notice specifically flags risk to young children, for whom Listeria infections can be serious or fatal. Product was distributed from the company's Renton, WA facility.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Brief safety alert for parenting outlets or local news in the Pacific Northwest. Limited national reach unless linked to confirmed illnesses, but worth a quick news brief or social post for family health channels with a "check your freezer" hook.
