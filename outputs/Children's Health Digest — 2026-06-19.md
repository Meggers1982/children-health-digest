## Advocacy & Nonprofits

### Autism Society of America
*Advocacy & Nonprofits*

**Disability and Education Groups Sound Alarm Over Transfer of Special Education Office from Education Department**
Published: 2026-06-18 | Source: Autism Society of America | [Link](https://autismsociety.org/broad-coalition-of-disability-civil-rights-and-education-organizations-denounces-eds-latest-transfers-of-core-functions/)

A broad coalition of disability, civil rights, and education organisations — including the Autism Society of America — is publicly opposing the Administration's plan to transfer the Office of Special Education and Rehabilitative Services (OSERS) out of the U.S. Department of Education. OSERS administers IDEA (Individuals with Disabilities Education Act) funding and oversees enforcement of special education rights for millions of children with disabilities. Advocates warn the move could weaken federal oversight and fragment services.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, Today's Parent, or disability parenting blogs as an explainer: "What happens to your child's IEP if special education oversight moves out of the Education Department?" Frame around what parents of children with disabilities need to know now — including how to document current services and advocate locally if federal enforcement weakens.
- **Trade/B2B:** Relevant for Education Week, Disability Scoop, or health policy trades — frame as analysis of what structural change at OSERS could mean for IDEA enforcement, state compliance, and school-based therapy services.

**KFF Launches Comprehensive Tracker for 2025 Reconciliation Law Medicaid Work Requirements**
Published: 2026-06-18 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link (main tracker)](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-work-requirements-tracker-overview/)

KFF has released an interactive tracker following implementation of Medicaid work requirements under the 2025 budget reconciliation law, set to take effect in January 2027. The tracker includes state-level data on Medicaid enrollment, renewal outcomes, 1115 waivers, and key implementation questions. This is a major resource for understanding how work requirements will roll out and which families may be most affected.

**New KFF Poll: 3 in 10 Adults Use Social Media for Health Advice — Few Verify What They See**
Published: 2026-06-17 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-tracking-poll-on-health-information-and-trust-use-of-social-media-and-ai-for-health-information-and-advice/)

A new KFF tracking poll finds about 3 in 10 U.S. adults turn to social media for health information and advice at least monthly, driven by the need for immediate answers and community connection. Slim majorities of social media health users say they're confident they can tell what's true — but relatively few take steps to verify what they see. A related interactive dashboard tracks trends in public trust, vaccine attitudes, and exposure to health misinformation.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Strong feature for Parents, Motherly, or Today's Parent: "Is the parenting advice you're getting on Instagram actually safe?" Use poll data to explore why parents turn to social media for health guidance, the risks of unverified advice (especially for infant feeding, vaccines, and child development), and how to vet what you read. Interview a pediatrician on red flags.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Pediatric News or Contemporary Pediatrics — frame as a practice management piece: "Your patients are getting health advice from TikTok. Here's how to talk to them about it."
