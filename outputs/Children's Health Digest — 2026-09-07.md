## Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
*Category: Government & Policy*

**Two Listeria Recalls Hit Deli Products: What Parents Need to Know About Ready-to-Eat Food Safety**
Published: 2026-09-06 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Pork Guanciale Recall](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766384) | [Deli Salads & Cream Cheese Recall](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766386)

Two separate recalls were announced for ready-to-eat products due to potential Listeria monocytogenes contamination. Prime Line Distributors and Ferrarini USA recalled approximately 1,513 pounds of imported pork guanciale, while Made Fresh Salads, Inc. recalled all of its deli-style salads and cream cheese products. Listeria poses serious risks to young children, pregnant women, and elderly individuals — causing potentially fatal infections. Parents should check refrigerators for these products and discard or return them immediately.
