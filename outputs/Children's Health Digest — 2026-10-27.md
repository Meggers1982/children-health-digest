**More Than Half of Women in Abortion-Ban States Don't Know Where to Access Care — A 10-Point Jump Since 2024**
Published: 2026-10-26 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/womens-health-policy/survey-more-than-half-of-women-in-states-with-abortion-bans-wouldnt-know-where-to-get-an-abortion-if-they-needed-one-up-since-2024/)

New KFF survey data finds 53% of reproductive-age women in states with abortion bans say they wouldn't know where to go or find information if they needed an abortion — up from 43% in 2024. The widening knowledge gap comes more than four years after Dobbs and suggests that confusion, not just legal barriers, is increasingly shaping access.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, Motherly, or NYT Well as a service piece: "What parents in abortion-ban states need to know now about emergency pregnancy care and information access." The 10-point jump in confusion is the hook — frame around what resources exist and what expectant parents should know before a crisis.
- **Trade/B2B:** Angle for Contemporary OB/GYN or health policy outlets on how clinicians in ban states are (or aren't) counseling patients, and the liability/communication challenges driving the knowledge gap.
