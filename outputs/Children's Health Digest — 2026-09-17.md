**Non-Dairy Frozen Dessert Recalled for Possible Foreign Material — Affects Products Sold Through 2028**
Published: 2026-09-15 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766462)

Danone USA is voluntarily recalling So Delicious Dairy Free® Salted Caramel Cluster pints (best-by dates on or before April 3, 2028) due to potential presence of small foreign materials. The recall affects a product commonly purchased by families managing dairy allergies or dietary restrictions in children.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Quick-hit safety alert for parenting outlets or allergy-focused blogs: "Check your freezer — popular dairy-free treat recalled for foreign material." Good fit for *Parents* safety roundups or *Consumer Reports* recall tracker. Include guidance on how to identify affected products and request refunds.
