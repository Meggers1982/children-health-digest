Published: 2026-11-10 | Source: Child Mind Institute | [Link](https://childmind.org/article/what-is-sextortion/)

The article opens with a case study of a 16-year-old boy who received a Snapchat message from what appeared to be a college student—a common setup in sextortion schemes that target minors.
