## Georgetown University Center for Children and Families
*Advocacy & Nonprofits*

**Child Uninsured Rate Stuck at Decade-High; Youngest Children Losing Ground**
Published: 2026-10-30 | Source: Georgetown Center for Children and Families (CCF) | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/10/30/new-census-data-show-child-uninsured-rate-hovers-at-highest-level-in-decade-increases-for-young-children/)

New 2025 American Community Survey data released by the U.S. Census Bureau show the child uninsured rate remains at its highest level in a decade, with particular deterioration among young children. The ACS data — delayed more than a month past the typical mid-September release — offer granular state and demographic breakdowns that supplement the earlier CPS analysis. The findings suggest the Medicaid unwinding and coverage losses that began in 2023 continue to drive a sustained coverage crisis for kids.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents or NYT Well as a "what parents need to know" explainer: Why are more kids — especially babies and toddlers — uninsured now than at any point in the last decade? Frame around actionable steps for families who may have lost coverage and don't realize they qualify for CHIP or Marketplace subsidies. Strong visual potential with state-by-state maps.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Pediatric News or Health Affairs Blog: Policy analysis on how the post-unwinding coverage landscape is affecting pediatric access, with implications for practice workflows and charity care.

**State Choices on Medicaid Work Requirements Creating a Patchwork of Verification Rules**
Published: 2026-10-29 | Source: Georgetown Center for Children and Families (CCF) | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/10/29/how-are-states-verifying-compliance-with-or-exclusion-from-work-reporting-requirements/)

Georgetown's latest analysis tracks how states are implementing verification systems for Medicaid work reporting requirements — one of the most operationally complex aspects of the new mandates. Policy decisions vary significantly, affecting whether applicants and enrollees can prove compliance or exemption. The analysis highlights how these administrative choices could become barriers to coverage for parents and caregivers.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Consumer Reports or a regional outlet in a state with active work requirements: "Is your state making it harder to keep Medicaid?" Frame as a checklist/explainer for working parents navigating new paperwork demands.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Contemporary Pediatrics or state medical society publications: How pediatricians can help families anticipate and respond to work-requirement verification issues that threaten children's coverage continuity.

## Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
*Government & Policy*

**Ground Cinnamon Recall Over Lead Contamination — Another Spice Safety Warning for Families**
Published: 2026-10-29 | Source: CDC | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766767)

SLR Food Distribution Inc. is voluntarily recalling Wise Wife Brand Ground Cinnamon due to elevated lead levels. The recall follows a string of lead-contaminated cinnamon incidents over the past two years that have prompted FDA and CDC warnings, including cases linked to children's applesauce pouches. Lead exposure is especially dangerous for young children, affecting brain development even at low levels.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, Today's Parent, or Good Housekeeping as a follow-up to the 2023–24 cinnamon-applesauce crisis: "Why does lead keep showing up in cinnamon — and what should parents do?" Include how to check pantry products, what symptoms to watch for, and whether blood lead testing is warranted.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Food Safety News or pediatric practice newsletters: Update on supply-chain vulnerabilities in imported spices and clinical guidance for pediatricians fielding parent questions.

## FRAXA Research Foundation
*Clinical & Research*

**Fragile X Drug Candidate Advances Toward Clinical Trials, Building on Foundation-Funded Science**
Published: 2026-10-28 | Source: FRAXA Research Foundation | [Link](https://www.fraxa.org/pde2-drug-program-builds-on-fraxa-funded-research/)

Rumi Therapeutics is advancing RTX-7340, a PDE2 inhibitor, toward clinical development for Fragile X syndrome — a genetic condition that is the leading inherited cause of intellectual disability and autism. The drug program builds directly on research funded by the FRAXA Research Foundation. If successful, it would represent a rare targeted therapy for a pediatric neurodevelopmental disorder with no FDA-approved treatments.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Spectrum News, Understood.org, or a parenting outlet focused on special needs: "A new drug for Fragile X is heading toward trials — here's what families should know." Emphasize the science-to-therapy pipeline and realistic timelines for availability.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Pediatric News or neurology trade outlets: Pipeline watch on PDE2 inhibitors as a therapeutic class for neurodevelopmental conditions; implications for genetic testing and early diagnosis protocols.
