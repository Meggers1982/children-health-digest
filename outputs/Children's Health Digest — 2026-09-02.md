### Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
*Government & Policy*

**Halloween Candy Recall: Undeclared Milk in "Chocolatey Eyeballs" Poses Risk for Kids with Allergies**
Published: 2026-08-31 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766349)

Crystal Temptations of North Arlington, NJ is recalling Halloween Chocolatey Eyeballs due to undeclared milk allergens. Children with milk allergies or severe sensitivities face risk of serious or life-threatening allergic reactions if they consume the product. With Halloween season approaching, this recall affects a product likely to appear in trick-or-treat bags and party candy dishes.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, Today's Parent, or parenting blogs as a timely Halloween safety alert — "Before You Buy Halloween Candy: One Product Already Recalled for Hidden Allergens." Include tips on checking labels and what to do if your child has already eaten the product. Strong fit for the "What Parents Need to Know" format.
