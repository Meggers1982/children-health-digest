## Children's Defense Fund
*Advocacy & Nonprofits*

**Trump Administration Proposal Would Cut SNAP Benefits for More Than 6 Million Children**
Published: 2026-11-13 | Source: Children's Defense Fund | [Link](https://www.childrensdefense.org/president-must-stop-making-children-targets-for-cuts-latest-proposal-would-strip-more-than-six-million-children-of-snap-benefits/)

The Trump administration proposed ending "Broad-Based Categorical Eligibility" (BBCE), which allows states to extend SNAP benefits to families who qualify for TANF cash assistance. If finalized, the rule change would strip food assistance from more than 6 million children. BBCE has been a key tool states use to simplify enrollment and reduce cliff effects for working families.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, NYT Well, or Today's Parent as an urgent explainer: "What the proposed SNAP rule means for your family" — walk parents through who's at risk, how to check eligibility, and what advocates say families should do now. Strong for a reader-service piece paired with a sidebar on child hunger data.
- **Trade/B2B:** Policy trade angle for Health Affairs or Politico Pro: How states that rely heavily on BBCE (e.g., those with high child enrollment in SNAP) would be disproportionately affected; potential downstream effects on pediatric nutrition outcomes and Medicaid enrollment.

## Georgetown University Center for Children and Families
*Advocacy & Nonprofits*

**New Advocate's Guide Explains How Medicaid Managed Care Spending Rules Affect Kids**
Published: 2026-11-13 | Source: Georgetown CCF | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/11/13/advocates-guide-to-medical-loss-ratios-in-medicaid-managed-care/)

Georgetown CCF released a detailed guide to Medical Loss Ratios (MLR) in Medicaid managed care — the percentage of premium dollars that insurers must spend on actual care versus administration and profit. With over 40% of Medicaid's 66 million enrollees being children, the guide is designed to help advocates push for better accountability from managed care organizations. This is the third in CCF's series on MCO oversight.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Limited direct consumer angle, but could support a broader investigative or explainer piece on "Where does your child's Medicaid dollar actually go?" for a health policy-adjacent consumer outlet or newsletter.
- **Trade/B2B:** Strong fit for Pediatric News, Modern Healthcare, or a state health policy outlet: How MLR enforcement (or lack thereof) affects pediatric care quality in Medicaid managed care states; which states are falling short and what advocates are demanding.

## KFF

**Rural Health Fund Awards Still Vary Wildly — Some States Get 5x More Per Resident**
Published: 2026-11-13 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/second-year-rural-health-fund-awards-per-rural-resident-continue-to-vary-widely/)

KFF's analysis of Year 2 Rural Health Fund awards shows stark disparities persist: 12 states receive less than $100 per rural resident, while 8 states receive more than $500. The variation raises questions about whether children in rural areas are getting equitable access to health resources depending on where they live.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to regional outlets or Consumer Reports as "Does your state shortchange rural kids' health funding?" — map the disparity and explain what it means for families in underfunded states (longer drives to care, fewer pediatric specialists, etc.).
- **Trade/B2B:** For Hospitals & Health Networks or a rural health policy outlet: Why does funding vary so much, and what are the implications for rural pediatric infrastructure?

Published: 2026-11-13 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-waiver-tracker-approved-and-pending-section-1115-waivers-by-state/)
