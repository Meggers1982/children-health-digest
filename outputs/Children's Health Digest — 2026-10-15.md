**Federal ban on Medicaid/CHIP funding for gender-affirming care for minors takes effect**
Published: 2026-10-13 | Source: Center for the Study of Social Policy (CSSP) | [Link](https://cssp.org/statement-on-implementation-of-cms-rule-prohibiting-federal-medicaid-and-chip-funding-for-gender-affirming-medication-and-surgical-care/)

The CMS final rule prohibiting federal Medicaid and CHIP funding for gender-affirming medications and surgical care for minors went into effect October 13. **SSI applicants at risk of losing Medicaid under new work requirements**
Published: 2026-10-14 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/what-could-medicaid-work-requirements-mean-for-ssi-applicants/)

KFF analysis finds that Supplemental Security Income (SSI) applicants — many of whom have disabilities — may struggle to maintain Medicaid coverage under new federal work requirements due to the complexity of proving eligibility for a "medical frailty" exemption. **SSDI beneficiaries in Medicare waiting period could lose Medicaid**
Published: 2026-10-14 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/what-could-medicaid-work-requirements-mean-for-people-with-disabilities-waiting-for-medicare-coverage/)

KFF finds Medicaid covered over 1 in 4 SSDI applicants and beneficiaries (120,300 of 415,300 in 2023) during the two-year waiting period before Medicare eligibility kicks in. New work requirements may jeopardize coverage for this group, which includes parents with disabilities and young adults aging out of pediatric care systems.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to *AARP* family caregiving section or *Consumer Reports* health: The hidden gap in disability coverage — what happens when you qualify for SSDI but have to wait two years for Medicare? Now, Medicaid work rules add a new risk.
- **Trade/B2B:** For *Health Affairs* blog or policy trades: Analysis of the compounding coverage gaps facing people with disabilities — SSI/SSDI waiting periods plus new Medicaid work requirements.

**ACA Marketplace enrollment drops 18% — over 4.6 million people lost coverage in one year**
Published: 2026-10-14 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/individual-market-enrollment-continues-to-fall-through-mid-2026/)

Individual market enrollment fell from 25.2 million in June 2025 to 20.5 million in June 2026 — an 18% decline. KFF notes this signals further erosion of ACA Marketplace coverage, which includes millions of families with children. The drop follows the expiration of enhanced premium subsidies and ongoing Medicaid unwinding.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to *Consumer Reports* or *The New York Times* Well: 4.6 million people dropped off individual health plans in one year — what's driving the exodus, and what should families do now if they're uninsured or underinsured heading into open enrollment?
- **Trade/B2B:** For *Modern Healthcare* or health policy outlets: Data-driven analysis of ACA erosion and implications for pediatric coverage as families fall out of both Medicaid and Marketplace plans.
