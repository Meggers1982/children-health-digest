## Center for the Study of Social Policy
*[Category: Advocacy & Nonprofits]*

**Federal ban on Medicaid/CHIP funding for youth gender-affirming care takes effect**
Published: 2026-10-13 | Source: Center for the Study of Social Policy (CSSP) | [Link](https://cssp.org/statement-on-implementation-of-cms-rule-prohibiting-federal-medicaid-and-chip-funding-for-gender-affirming-medication-and-surgical-care/)

The CMS final rule prohibiting federal Medicaid and CHIP funding for gender-affirming medications and surgical care for minors went into effect October 13. CSSP's statement argues this directly contradicts the core purpose of Medicaid and CHIP — supporting children's health. The rule affects low-income transgender youth who rely on these programs for coverage; states may still use state-only funds, but access will vary dramatically by state.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to NYT Well, Parents, or a LGBTQ+ family outlet: "What the new federal ban means for families of trans kids on Medicaid." Service angle — explain what care is now affected, which states may still cover it with state funds, and what options remain for families. Strong human-interest opportunity with affected families.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Pediatric News or health policy trades: analysis of how pediatricians in Medicaid-heavy practices are navigating the new coverage gap, and what professional societies are advising.

## KFF
*[Category: Government & Policy]*

**Individual health insurance enrollment dropped 18% in one year — 4.6 million people lost coverage**
Published: 2026-10-14 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/individual-market-enrollment-continues-to-fall-through-mid-2026/)

Enrollment in the ACA individual market fell from 25.2 million (June 2025) to 20.5 million (June 2026) — an 18% drop affecting 4.6 million people. This is a steeper decline than previously reported and reflects both on-exchange Marketplace plans and off-exchange individual coverage. The data raises urgent questions about where these families are going: employer coverage, Medicaid, or uninsured status.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Consumer Reports, NYT Upshot, or health sections: "4.6 million Americans lost individual health coverage this year — here's what that means for families." Investigate how many of these are children/families losing coverage, and what safety-net options remain. Frame as open-enrollment guidance.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Modern Healthcare or health policy trades: analysis of what's driving the ACA enrollment decline (premium subsidy expiration, Medicaid churn, economic factors) and implications for pediatric coverage rates.
