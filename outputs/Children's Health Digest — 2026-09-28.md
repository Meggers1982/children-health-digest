### Autism Society of America
*Advocacy & Nonprofits*

**Autism Society partners with pool safety org to address drowning risk in autistic children**
Published: 2026-09-28 | Source: Autism Society of America | [Link](https://autismsociety.org/a-safepool-news/)

The Autism Society of America's AuQUA Life program has announced a partnership with A Safe Pool, a pool safety organization, to focus on drowning prevention for autistic individuals. Drowning is a leading cause of death for autistic children, who often face heightened risks due to wandering behavior and sensory differences around water. The collaboration aims to combine autism-specific expertise with pool safety resources to create layered prevention strategies.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, Today's Parent, or a parenting blog as a summer safety/year-round awareness piece: "Why autistic children face higher drowning risks — and what parents can do now." Include practical guidance on water barriers, supervision strategies, and swim instruction tailored for autistic kids. Could also work as a post for special-needs parenting outlets or as a sidebar to any broader pool safety coverage.
- **Trade/B2B:** Brief mention for pediatric practice newsletters or disability advocacy trade outlets covering community partnerships in safety programming.

Published: 2026-09-28 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/understanding-the-role-of-medicaid-fraud-control-units-mfcus/)

KFF released a policy brief describing the role of Medicaid Fraud Control Units (MFCUs), including data on caseloads, case outcomes, and emerging issues facing these enforcement bodies. **Mezzetta recalls peperoncini jars due to pest contamination**
Published: 2026-09-26 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766538)

G.L. Mezzetta Inc. issued a voluntary recall of Mezzetta Golden Greek Peperoncini (Medium Heat, 32 oz) after a pest contaminant was found in jars. The recall is limited to a specific lot. No illnesses have been reported. Families with the product should check lot numbers and discard or return affected jars.
