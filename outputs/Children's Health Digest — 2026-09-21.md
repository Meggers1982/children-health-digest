**Medicaid Managed Care Plans May Exit States as Rate-Setting Rules Create Uncertainty**
Published: 2026-09-21 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/implementation-of-2025-reconciliation-law-medicaid-managed-care-rate-setting-uncertainty-potential-plan-exits/)

KFF's new policy watch examines how implementation of the 2025 reconciliation law is creating significant uncertainty around Medicaid managed care rate setting — with early signs that some managed care organizations (MCOs) may exit state markets. For the roughly 40 million children enrolled in Medicaid, MCO exits could mean disrupted provider networks, loss of established pediatrician relationships, and gaps in coverage continuity. **28.5 Million Americans Face Health Barriers Due to Limited English — Children in LEP Families at Higher Risk**
Published: 2026-09-21 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/racial-equity-and-health-policy/overview-of-health-coverage-and-care-for-individuals-with-limited-english-proficiency/)

A new KFF overview finds approximately 28.5 million U.S. residents ages 5+ have limited English proficiency (LEP), with disproportionate gaps in insurance coverage and poorer health outcomes driven partly by language barriers. Because LEP status is more common among people of color, these access barriers compound existing racial and ethnic health disparities. Children in LEP households may face added obstacles accessing pediatric care, school-based health services, and preventive screenings.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Consumer Reports Health or NYT Well as a service piece: "How to navigate your child's healthcare when English isn't your first language" — cover rights to interpreter services, tips for ER and clinic visits, and how to advocate for language access at schools. Strong human-interest opportunity by profiling a family's experience.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Contemporary Pediatrics or AAP News: How are pediatric practices meeting (or failing) language access requirements? What's the ROI on medical interpreter services for patient retention and outcomes in diverse practices?
