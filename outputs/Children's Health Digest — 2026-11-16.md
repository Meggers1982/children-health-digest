**New Research Proposes Framework for Validating AI Tools Used in Children's Mental Health Assessments**
Published: 2026-11-16 | Source: Child Mind Institute | [Link](https://childmind.org/blog/new-research-for-stronger-standards-when-using-large-language-models-for-psychological-assessment/)

The Child Mind Institute has published new research calling for stronger validation standards when large language models and other AI tools are used for psychological assessment in children and adolescents. The work proposes a framework for evaluating AI effectiveness in mental health settings, amid growing use of AI-assisted screening and diagnostic tools in pediatric behavioral health.
