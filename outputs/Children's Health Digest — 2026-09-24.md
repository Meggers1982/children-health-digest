**New Medicaid/CHIP restrictions for immigrant children take effect October 1 — what families and states need to know**
Published: 2026-09-22 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/immigrant-health/implementation-of-medicaid-immigrant-eligibility-restrictions-under-the-2025-reconciliation-law-issues-to-consider/)

The 2025 reconciliation law imposes new eligibility restrictions for many lawfully present immigrants in Medicaid and CHIP, effective October 1, 2026 — just days away. KFF's implementation brief outlines CMS guidance and flags key issues for affected individuals and states, including documentation requirements, coverage gaps, and transition concerns for children currently enrolled.

**Health care costs force women to delay care, ration meds, and cut basic spending, new KFF survey finds**

The 2026 KFF Women's Health Survey finds health care costs remain a major barrier for women ages 18–64, leading to delayed care, skipped or rationed medications, and cuts to spending on necessities like food and housing. The survey provides nationally representative data on affordability struggles affecting mothers and caregivers.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, Motherly, or consumer health outlets: "How health care costs are forcing moms to choose between their own care and family basics." Data-driven service piece with expert tips on navigating costs.
- **Trade/B2B:** Brief mention potential for Contemporary Pediatrics or family medicine trades on how affordability barriers affect maternal health — and, by extension, child health outcomes.

## Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
*Government & Policy*

**Alfalfa sprouting seed recall: potential E. coli and Salmonella contamination**
Published: 2026-09-23 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766511)

Berlin Seeds (Millersburg, OH) is recalling 950 lbs of alfalfa sprouting seeds due to potential contamination with Shiga toxin-producing E. coli (STEC) and/or Salmonella. Sprouts are a known high-risk food for children, pregnant women, and immunocompromised individuals.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Safety alert for Parents, What to Expect, or parenting blogs: "Why experts say kids and pregnant women should avoid raw sprouts — and what to do if you have these seeds at home." Timely peg with evergreen sprout safety info.
