Published: 2026-09-08 | Source: Georgetown Center for Children and Families (CCF) | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/09/08/access-to-maternity-care-in-rural-areas-likely-to-get-worse-due-to-medicaid-cuts/)

## FRAXA Research Foundation
*Category: Clinical & Research*

**New Fragile X Drug Enters Phase 2 Trial — Recruiting Boys and Men Ages 13–45**
Published: 2026-09-08 | Source: FRAXA Research Foundation | [Link](https://www.fraxa.org/mirum-clinical-trial-of-mrm-3379-for-fragile-x-syndrome/)

Mirum Pharmaceuticals has launched a Phase 2 clinical trial of MRM-3379, a PDE4D inhibitor, for Fragile X syndrome — the most common inherited cause of intellectual disability and autism. The trial is now enrolling males ages 13–45 at multiple U.S. sites. The drug candidate emerged from FRAXA-funded basic research, marking a bench-to-bedside milestone for the rare disease community.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to *Parents* special-needs section, disability-focused outlets, or autism/rare disease blogs as a "trial alert" piece: what families should know about eligibility, what the drug targets, and how to find a study site. Include context on the Fragile X treatment landscape (currently no FDA-approved drugs for the core syndrome).
- **Trade/B2B:** For *Pediatric News* or pharma trades: Phase 2 launch as a signal of renewed commercial interest in Fragile X after years of failed trials; note the PDE4D mechanism and what differentiates this candidate.
