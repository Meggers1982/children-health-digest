**Nebraska's Medicaid Work Requirements Are Already Causing Coverage Losses, First State Data Shows**
Published: 2026-10-01 | Source: Georgetown Center for Children and Families (CCF) | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/09/30/nebraska-releases-first-data-on-medicaid-work-reporting-requirement-as-expected-coverage-losses-start-to-mount-due-to-red-tape/)

Nebraska, the first state to implement the new mandatory Medicaid work reporting requirements, has released its first data showing coverage losses are mounting due to paperwork burdens. The state began enforcing rules for new applicants earlier this year, ahead of the January 2027 national mandate. Data was presented at a recent Medicaid Advisory Committee meeting.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** This is the first real-world evidence of what's coming for Medicaid families nationwide. Pitch to Parents or NYT Well: "Your family's Medicaid coverage could depend on paperwork—here's what Nebraska's experience tells us about the coming changes." Frame as a family prep/know-your-rights explainer for parents in Medicaid households.
- **Trade/B2B:** First-look data story for Contemporary Pediatrics or health policy trades on early implementation challenges and what pediatric practices should expect as work requirements roll out nationally.

**Federal Government Changes How It Counts Measles Deaths Amid Ongoing Outbreak**
Published: 2026-10-01 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/other-health/how-is-the-federal-government-counting-measles-deaths/)

HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. has changed how the federal government tracks deaths in its official measles tally following Pennsylvania's report of four deaths.
