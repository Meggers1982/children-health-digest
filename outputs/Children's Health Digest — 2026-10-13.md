**California Adds Science to School Accountability Dashboard After Decade-Long Campaign**
Published: 2026-10-13 | Source: Children Now | [Link](https://www.childrennow.org/blog/science-california-school-dashboard-decade-of-advocacy/)

California has added science assessment results to its official school accountability dashboard, a change Children Now credits to 10 years of sustained advocacy. The move means science performance will now factor into how schools are evaluated alongside math and English language arts — potentially shifting resources and attention toward STEM instruction in underperforming districts. **Podcast: AI in Health Care Risks Deepening Inequities, Says Health Equity Expert**
Published: 2026-10-13 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/other-health/ai-in-health-care-who-gets-left-behind/)

In a new KFF podcast episode, Dr. Uché Blackstock argues that AI tools being deployed in health care risk replicating or worsening existing disparities unless affected communities have a seat at the governance table. Blackstock — author of *Legacy: A Black Physician Reckons with Racism in Medicine* — calls for AI oversight to extend beyond regulators to include patients and community voices.
