## Child Mind Institute

**New Survey: Where Teens Actually Feel They Belong — and Why It Matters for Their Mental Health**
Published: 2026-10-07 | Source: Child Mind Institute | [Link](https://childmind.org/blog/child-mind-institute-study-finds-strong-link-between-teen-belonging-and-mental-health/)

Released ahead of World Mental Health Day (Oct. 10), the report "Building Bridges: Teen Loneliness and Connection" surveyed 1,000 U.S. teens aged 14–17 on what connection means to them, where they feel most connected, and who they turn to for support. The study establishes a direct link between a teen's sense of belonging and their mental health outcomes.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents or NYT Well as a practical guide: "Where do teens actually feel they belong — and what can parents do about it?" Lead with the survey's findings on where teens feel most/least connected (home? school? online?), then pair with expert advice on how parents can foster belonging without hovering. Strong World Mental Health Day news hook.
- **Trade/B2B:** For Contemporary Pediatrics or school counseling outlets: how pediatricians and school staff can screen for belonging/loneliness as a mental health indicator.

**AI Tool Uses Language Patterns to Predict Suicide Risk in Crisis Texts**
Published: 2026-10-05 | Source: Child Mind Institute | [Link](https://childmind.org/blog/using-large-language-models-to-create-lexicons-for-interpretable-text-models-with-high-content-validity-the-suicide-risk-lexicon/)

Researchers developed a language-processing tool that analyzes words and phrases in text-based crisis counseling conversations to estimate imminent suicide risk. Tested on real crisis conversations, the model accurately predicted risk and identified which specific language patterns were most strongly associated with danger.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** For a tech-meets-parenting piece in Wired or a health section: "Could AI help spot when a teen is in crisis? What parents should know about new suicide-detection tools" — frame as both promising and raising questions about privacy and accuracy.
- **Trade/B2B:** Pitch to Pediatric News or crisis-intervention outlets on clinical implications: how AI-assisted text analysis could support school counselors or pediatric mental health screening.

**LGBTQ 988 Crisis Line Restored After Trump-Era Shutdown — But Questions Remain**
Published: 2026-10-05 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/lgbtq/as-hhs-restores-the-lgbtq-988-suicide-and-crisis-line-under-pressure-from-congress-questions-remain/)

HHS has restored the LGBTQ-specific 988 suicide and crisis line after it was shut down in July 2025. The service had been launched in 2023 to meet the acute mental health needs of LGBTQ young people; utilization was rising at the time of its shutdown. KFF's policy watch details what happened and what gaps remain.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to teen/LGBTQ-focused outlets or NYT Well: "What parents of LGBTQ teens should know about the 988 crisis line — and what's changed." Lead with the human stakes: LGBTQ youth are at significantly higher risk for suicidal ideation, and this service was designed specifically for them.
- **Trade/B2B:** For health policy or mental health trades: what the on-again, off-again policy means for crisis intervention infrastructure and continuity of care.
