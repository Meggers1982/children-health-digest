## FRAXA Research Foundation
*Clinical & Research*

**Researchers Test Novel RNA Approach to Restore Missing Protein in Fragile X Syndrome**
Published: 2026-09-17 | Source: FRAXA Research Foundation | [Link](https://www.fraxa.org/circular-rna-delivery-of-fmrp-in-a-human-neurodevelopmental-model-of-fragile-x-syndrome/?utm_source=rss&utm_medium=rss&utm_campaign=circular-rna-delivery-of-fmrp-in-a-human-neurodevelopmental-model-of-fragile-x-syndrome)

FRAXA-funded researchers are exploring circular RNA as a delivery method to restore FMRP — the protein missing in children with Fragile X syndrome, the leading inherited cause of intellectual disability and autism. Unlike viral gene therapies, this non-viral approach could potentially allow repeat dosing, addressing a key limitation of current experimental treatments. The research is in human neurodevelopmental models, signaling early but promising progress toward clinical application.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** For families affected by Fragile X, this represents a new therapeutic strategy that could eventually mean ongoing treatment rather than one-shot gene therapy. Pitch to disability/special needs parenting outlets or health sections (NYT Well, STAT) as a "what's next in Fragile X research" explainer — frame around what this could mean for the roughly 1 in 4,000 boys born with the condition.
- **Trade/B2B:** Pitch to genetic medicine or pediatric neurology trades (e.g., Neurology Today, Pediatric News) as an update on non-viral gene therapy platforms in neurodevelopmental disorders.

## March of Dimes

**March of Dimes and Blank Family Foundation Partner to Fund Maternal and Infant Mental Health Startups**
Published: 2026-09-16 | Source: March of Dimes | [Link](https://www.marchofdimes.org/about/news/march-dimes-and-ambff-partner-to-advance-maternal-mental-health)

The March of Dimes Innovation Fund has received philanthropic backing from the Arthur M. Blank Family Foundation to invest in early-stage companies focused on maternal and infant mental health. The partnership aims to accelerate solutions for perinatal mood disorders and early infant bonding/mental health challenges — conditions that affect an estimated 1 in 5 new mothers and have downstream effects on child development.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch as a trend piece on the growing investment pipeline for maternal mental health tech and services — what's being funded, what tools might reach new parents soon, and why this space is finally getting serious attention. Good fit for parenting outlets (Parents, What to Expect) or women's health sections.
