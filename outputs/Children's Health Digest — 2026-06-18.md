**Federal Medicaid State Directed Payments Reach $93 Billion Annually; New Limits Will Cut Funding to 41 States**
Published: 2026-06-15 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/federal-medicaid-spending-through-state-directed-payments-nears-100-billion-annually-across-41-states-with-new-limits-set-to-reduce-funding-to-states/)

KFF estimates that 40 states plus DC currently receive $93 billion annually in federal Medicaid spending through state directed payments (SDPs), with California leading at $10.6 billion, followed by Texas ($6.3B), North Carolina ($5.2B), and Illinois ($5.1B). These payments are now at risk due to forthcoming limits under the 2025 reconciliation law, which reduced federal Medicaid spending by an estimated $911 billion over 2025–2034.

**Flag:** 🔴 Action required
Children's hospitals relying on SDP-funded supplemental payments should immediately assess exposure; this will directly affect pediatric Medicaid reimbursement in most states.
