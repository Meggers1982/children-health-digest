**New Tracker: How States Are Preparing for Medicaid Work Requirements Starting January 2027**
Published: 2026-11-03 | Source: KFF | [Overview](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-work-requirements-tracker-overview/) | [State Data](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-work-requirements-tracker-state-national-data-and-policies/) | [1115 Waivers](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-work-requirements-tracker-1115-waivers/) | [Implementation Questions](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-work-requirements-tracker-implementation-questions/) | [Resources](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-work-requirements-tracker-resources/)

KFF has launched an interactive tracker monitoring state-by-state implementation of the Medicaid work requirements mandated by the 2025 budget reconciliation law, which take effect in January 2027. The tracker includes current enrollment and renewal data, state policy variations, and a detailed list of unresolved implementation questions. For families, this is the clearest early-warning tool for how their state's approach to work requirements may affect coverage continuity — particularly for parents whose Medicaid eligibility is tied to their children's coverage.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Consumer Reports, Verywell Family, or a regional news outlet as a "what parents need to know now" explainer — which states are moving aggressively on work requirements, what documentation will families need, and what's the risk of coverage gaps for kids if a parent loses eligibility? Use the tracker to localize for a specific state.
- **Trade/B2B:** Pitch to Health Affairs Blog, Pediatric News, or a state health policy outlet as a deep-dive on the implementation questions that remain unresolved — e.g., how will states handle parents who lose eligibility, and what happens to children's continuous coverage guarantees?
