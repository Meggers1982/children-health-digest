## Georgetown University Center for Children and Families
*[Category: Advocacy & Nonprofits]*

**2.56 Million Children Have Lost Medicaid/CHIP Since January 2025 — Many Also Losing SNAP**
Published: 2026-11-09 | Source: Georgetown Center for Children and Families (CCF) | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/11/09/millions-of-low-income-children-are-losing-both-medicaid-chip-coverage-and-snap-benefits/)

Georgetown CCF's latest analysis of state administrative data through August 2026 finds that 2.56 million children have lost Medicaid and CHIP coverage across all states since January 2025. The brief highlights that many of these same families are simultaneously losing SNAP (food assistance) benefits, creating a compounding crisis where low-income children face gaps in both healthcare and nutrition support at once.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to Parents, NYT Well, or Today's Parent as a "what families need to know" service piece: If you're a parent who recently lost Medicaid or CHIP for your child, here's how to check if you're still eligible, how to appeal, and what stopgap options exist (community health centers, marketplace plans, emergency Medicaid). Include real family voices — CCF or state-level advocates can likely connect you with affected parents. Frame: "2.5 million kids lost coverage this year. Is yours one of them?"
- **Trade/B2B:** For Pediatric News or health policy outlets: How are pediatric practices managing the surge in uninsured children? What are FQHCs seeing? Policy angle on whether congressional action is imminent given new Democratic majority.
