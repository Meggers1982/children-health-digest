**Analysis: Data Used to Justify Medicaid Work Requirements Contains Critical Methodological Flaws**
Published: 2026-09-09 | Source: Georgetown Center for Children and Families (CCF) | [Link](https://ccf.georgetown.edu/2026/09/09/right-wing-data-arguing-for-medicaid-work-reporting-requirements-is-fatally-flawed/)

Georgetown CCF has published a detailed critique of research from the Foundation for Government Accountability (FGA) that has been used to support Medicaid work reporting requirements at federal and state levels. The analysis argues that FGA's methodology is "fatally flawed" and that the data does not support claims that work requirements improve outcomes. This comes as states implement provisions from the 2025 Reconciliation Law that mandated work requirements for certain Medicaid enrollees.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Pitch to outlets like Vox, The Atlantic's health section, or NYT's Upshot as an explainer on the data wars behind Medicaid work requirements — what do parents with children on Medicaid need to know about whether these policies actually help or hurt families? Frame as: "The fight over whether Medicaid work requirements help or harm families just got a new flashpoint."
- **Trade/B2B:** Pitch to Health Affairs Blog or Modern Healthcare as a policy analysis piece on how competing research claims are shaping state implementation of the 2025 law's work requirement provisions.

## KFF
*Clinical & Research*

**New Tracker: State-by-State Data on Medicaid Work Requirements Implementation**
Published: 2026-09-09 | Source: KFF Children's Health | [Link](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/medicaid-work-requirements-tracker-state-national-data-and-policies/)

KFF has launched a tracker providing detailed state and national data on how Medicaid work requirements from the 2025 Reconciliation Law are being implemented. The tool includes state-level policy details and enrollment data. Work requirements have historically led to coverage losses, including among parents — Arkansas's 2018 pilot saw over 18,000 people lose coverage, many of whom were working but failed to report hours.

**Recall: Broccoli Sprouts Pulled Over Salmonella Risk — Young Children at High Risk**
Published: 2026-09-10 | Source: CDC Children's Health | [Link](https://tools.cdc.gov/api/embed/downloader/download.asp?m=316422&c=766415)

Evergreen Fresh Sprouts LLC is recalling 215 cases of broccoli sprouts (6/4oz bags per case) distributed from Moyie Springs, Idaho, due to potential Salmonella contamination. The CDC notes Salmonella can cause "serious and sometimes fatal infections in young children." Parents should check refrigerators and discard affected product.

**📰 Story angles:**
- **Consumer:** Quick-hit safety alert for Parents, Today's Parent, or local news: "Broccoli sprouts recall: What parents need to know about Salmonella risk in kids." Include symptoms to watch for, what to do if child consumed product, and reminder that sprouts are a higher-risk food for young children generally.
